20 each of angular_leaf_spot, bean_rust, and healthy.
Changes from the original dataset: subset selection only. Original image bytes
and all source classification labels are retained without modification.
The preparation does not resize, crop, re-compress or rewrite image metadata.
This is a small workflow sample, not the complete dataset or its official split.

Imported labels remain import-unmodified until user edits (then import-edited).
Their provenance does not automatically confirm them or make them Ground Truth.
For workflow demonstration, not agricultural diagnosis or an official benchmark.
